## Grammar and fingerprint gate

**Grammar:** Rhythmic cutlist. This best fits the requested commercial pace. Filmic one-shot was rejected because the owner asked for bold cuts and a return to the old static hero rather than another continuous scrub narrative. Split stage is the rejected build. Chaptered editorial is too literary, live surface is too product-like, continuous world contradicts the scene cuts, typographic poster would lose the requested old hero, gallery/catalog overweights portfolio browsing, and split stage repeats the rejected structure.

Against `brayroai-signal-fold`, this differs on all six gated dimensions: rhythmic-cutlist grammar, loud glass broadcast bar, monochrome portrait colour-matte hero, twelve short flow/reveal cuts, abrupt cobalt end card, and the director's colour-matte signature.
