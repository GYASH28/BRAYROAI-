BEFORE YOU PICK.

Which option should I choose if I am not sure?

Start with the job you need done now. If the site mainly needs a professional reset, Digital Makeover is the leanest entry. If you need a complete business website, Full Website is the clearest middle option. If the experience needs custom interaction, integrations or product-style complexity, use Bespoke.

Can I switch to a monthly plan after the website launches?

Yes. The one-time build and monthly support paths are separate on purpose. You can finish a project first, then move into Launch, Grow or Pro if ongoing improvement makes sense.

Why is Grow highlighted?

It is positioned as the balanced ongoing option: enough room for meaningful iteration without jumping straight to the highest-touch Pro relationship. It is a recommendation based on scope design—not a fake popularity claim.

Are hosting and third-party subscriptions included?

Not automatically. If a project needs hosting, a paid service, a domain or another external tool, that cost is kept separate and visible instead of being hidden inside the plan price.
