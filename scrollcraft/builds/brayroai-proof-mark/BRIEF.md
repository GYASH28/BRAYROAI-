# BRAYROAI: The Proof Mark

Self-authored from the user's direct instructions and approved plan. The user asked for an autonomous build without more questions. This is a working production brief, not a claim that a new interview happened.

## User direction

1. **Vibe and references.** “Awards-level”; creative, animated, parallax-rich, refined. Preserve the original hero screen. Use Scrollcraft, Taste, UI/UX Pro Max and current web references.
2. **Journey.** Keep the hero, then make the rest of the site a new experience. Show real work, method, founder, options and a clear way to start.
3. **Energy.** A strong opening; varied motion through the site; animation in small controls as well as large scenes. The whole site should remain polished.
4. **Feeling and peak.** Recognition in the original hero, confidence in real work, curiosity about decisions, surprise when pieces assemble, intimacy with the founder, readiness to contact.
5. **Signature.** Bring back the custom hand-drawn orange arrow. It directs actual screen fragments into a finished work view and resolves into the case-study link.
6. **Aesthetic range.** High-variance creative studio with a photographic base, warm editorial surfaces, restrained signal orange and no generic card grid.
7. **Structure.** Distinct scenes and cuts, not one unbroken camera flight.
8. **Assets.** Existing original Yash hero cutouts, background and real FakhriMart desktop/mobile screenshots on GitHub main. No invented client results or metrics.

## Visitor promise

BRAYROAI makes distinctive digital work through clear decisions and real implementation. One action: **Start a project**.

## Page grammar

**Proof table.** The hero introduces the founder; each later scene presents a different piece of real work or a decision that shaped it. Navigation is a compact editorial index. The close is a real brief field and direct contact choices. Hard changes in material and scale separate scenes. No broadcast timecodes, continuous flythrough, repeated 01/06 counters, fake UI, fabricated testimonials or decorative feature cards.

This is separate from the local Split Stage and Rhythmic Cutlist fingerprints on all six dimensions: grammar, navigation, original layered hero, six-scene flow/pan/reveal/pin/flow sequence, held brief-field close and arrow-led assembly signature.

## Feeling curve, written before the device score

1. **Recognition:** the original founder hero arrives with photographic depth.
2. **Confidence:** a genuine client site fills an expansive warm surface.
3. **Curiosity:** details of real design decisions become visible in sequence.
4. **Surprise:** the hand-drawn arrow brings separate parts into an assembled live-looking view. This is the peak and gets the longest span.
5. **Intimacy:** motion settles around the real founder portrait and plain explanation.
6. **Resolve:** actual starting points and a brief field turn interest into contact.

Peak sentence: “The orange hand-drawn arrow moved the actual work pieces together, and suddenly the whole client site was there.”

Tell-someone sentence: “It is the site where the founder's orange arrow assembles the real work in front of you.”
