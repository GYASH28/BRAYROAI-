# BRAYROAI Director's Cut build report

## Outcome

The rejected split-stage site was replaced with a twelve-cut SaaS-commercial experience and a dedicated nine-cut plans film. The opening reconstructs the strongest composition from the previous homepage, turns it monochrome and gives the visitor a live director's colour matte. A new slate-and-curtain opening, staggered headline reveal, continuous progress interpolation and velocity-shaped transitions make the site feel edited rather than merely scrolled.

The interaction upgrade makes the commercial demonstrative rather than merely animated. The visitor can switch the real FakhriMart work between desktop and mobile, and that decision carries into the later proof composition. The brand film exposes play, pause and a directable timeline. The plans film recommends one of three corrected build paths, supports click and arrow-key direction, and carries the decision to the relevant scope. Every cut publishes smoothed local progress, while scroll velocity raises and settles the broadcast energy without obscuring the work.

## Grammar

Rhythmic cutlist. Every scene is a short flow act with a hard ground change. There are no pinned sections, no dwell and no continuous filmic world. The full-frame brand film is the peak, while the bone-white relief scene immediately before it provides authored silence.

The other grammars lost because they either repeat the rejected split architecture, require a continuous journey, remove the old photographic hero, or slow the commercial pace into editorial chapters or a product demo.

## Signature move

The Director's Colour Matte duplicates the real old hero composition into monochrome and colour layers. Pointer movement reveals colour through a live photographic aperture. A click, tap or keyboard-operable control locks the full grade, changes the hero statement and turns the visitor from viewer into director.

Supporting interactions now form a trace through the edit: a responsive viewport choice made in the web scene visibly changes the later proof scene, so the site remembers an authored decision instead of resetting at every cut.
