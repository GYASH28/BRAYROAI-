# BRAYROAI — DESIGN.md

> Canonical visual and interaction direction for the BRAYROAI website.

## North star

**INTELLIGENT CRAFT.**

BRAYROAI should feel like editorial restraint × digital energy: minimal enough to feel controlled, expressive enough to be memorable, and technically precise without falling into generic AI aesthetics.

## Brand structure

- Masterbrand: **BRAYROAI**
- Descriptor: **Creative Technology Studio**
- Promise: **Design. Engineering. AI.**
- Hero line: **Digital, designed to feel different.**
- Supporting line: **Premium digital experiences and intelligent systems.**

## Core principles

1. Clarity over clutter.
2. Depth over decoration.
3. Motion with purpose.
4. Craft over templates.
5. Intelligence without gimmicks.
6. Proof over claims.
7. Premium without pretension.

## Palette

- Ink `#09090B`
- Surface `#111318`

- Bone `#F3F0EA`
- Bone 2 `#E7E2D9`

- Soft Blue `#AFC2FF`
- Signal Orange `#FF6B2C`
- Soft Orange `#FFD2B8`

Blue and orange are signals, not wallpaper.

## Typography

- Display: Space Grotesk in the current implementation, with the intended character of a modern geometric display face.
- Body/UI: Manrope.
- Metadata: DM Mono.

Typography carries the identity. Headlines are large and tightly tracked; supporting copy stays calm and precise.

## Motion

Character: smooth, weighted, deliberate.

Use:
- mask reveals
- staggered entrances
- parallax depth
- sticky storytelling
- progress-linked transforms
- pointer response
- image/type overlap
- scale + opacity transitions

Avoid:
- bounce as a default
- random spinning
- permanent CPU-heavy motion
- scroll hijacking
- motion that hides content

`prefers-reduced-motion` must remove parallax and continuous effects rather than merely slowing them down.

## Capabilities

Exactly four primary disciplines:

1. Web Experiences
2. Product Design
3. Frontend Engineering
4. AI Systems

Interaction and motion are embedded in the disciplines rather than sold as a separate generic service.

## Project proof

Project sections must prioritize real interfaces and real deployments. No fake KPIs. Each featured project should communicate project name, category, role/status, one reason it matters, and a clear route to the live experience or repository.

## Accessibility + performance

- WCAG-aware contrast
- visible focus
- semantic headings
- meaningful alt text
- 44×44 touch targets where practical
- reduced-motion support
- LCP target below 2.5s on representative mobile conditions
- CLS below 0.1
- lazy-load non-critical embeds
- no viewport-dependent blank chapters

## Anti-patterns

Do not default to robot/brain imagery, neural dots, cyberpunk grids, sparkle icons, generic glass cards, excessive gradients, fake terminal filler, random 3D objects, or repeated template card sections.

## Final test

The result should feel **authored, premium, technical, alive, and unmistakably BRAYROAI**.
