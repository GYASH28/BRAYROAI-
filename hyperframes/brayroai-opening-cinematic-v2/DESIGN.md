# BRAYROAI HyperFrames Opening — Visual Identity

## Style Prompt
A premium cinematic studio ident for BRAYROAI. The film should feel like an expensive creative-technology studio title sequence rather than a SaaS template or AI demo. Use near-black space, warm bone typography, precise BRAYROAI orange accents, controlled photographic bloom, deep shadows, sparse editorial rules, and camera-like movement. Motion is heavy, tactile, elegant, seamless and confident. Every visible change should feel motivated by weight, rhythm, typography or sound.

## Colors
- `#070809` — primary near-black canvas
- `#F2EFE8` — warm bone primary typography
- `#FF5A1F` — BRAYROAI orange accent
- `#8D8982` — secondary warm gray metadata
- `#1A1D21` — deep structural shadow

## Typography
- Archivo Black — BRAYROAI wordmark and major display statements
- Manrope — secondary statement typography
- DM Mono — metadata, small labels and production marks

## Motion Language
- Heavy ease-outs with confident deceleration
- Short mechanical locks synchronized to tactile clicks
- Slow camera drift underneath fast micro-transients
- Motion blur suggested through duplicated edge slivers, not glow soup
- Transitions must flow continuously; no empty frames or jump-cut feeling
- Final frame should dissolve into near-black so the website hero can appear seamlessly

## Audio Language
- Deep 32–48 Hz sub-bass bed
- Rich mechanical clicks with low-mid body, not thin UI ticks
- Layered metal latch / shutter transients
- Restrained low-frequency whooshes
- One large final impact with sub tail
- No EDM, no generic trailer braams, no corporate swooshes

## What NOT to Do
- No generic AI particles, neural networks, glowing orbs or holographic HUDs
- No glassmorphism cards or dashboard visuals
- No neon sci-fi blue/purple palette
- No random glitch spam
- No cheap zoom-punch typography
- No stock footage
- No fake metrics
- No frantic cuts
