CASE 01 / REAL CLIENT / 2026

FAKHRIMART.

A digital catalogue and enquiry experience for a Pune yarn-and-craft business. The work focused on making a broad material range easier to discover, easier to understand on mobile, and easier to turn into a real enquiry.

CLIENTFakhriMart
BUSINESSYarn & craft materials
SCOPEExperience / frontend / deployment
STATUSLive website
THE BUSINESS JOB

MAKE THE
CATALOGUE
FEEL EASY.

FakhriMart is not a one-product brand. The website has to make yarns, shades, craft materials and project-oriented discovery feel navigable without pretending the business is a conventional click-to-cart ecommerce store.

The design direction therefore centres on discovery, visual trust and enquiry. Search, catalogue browsing, product shades, saved items and WhatsApp contact work together as the path from interest to conversation.

DESIGN DECISIONS

THREE THINGS
THAT MATTERED.

The case study avoids invented conversion percentages. These are the concrete experience decisions visible in the shipped interface.

01 / FIND

Put material discovery near the surface.

Search and catalogue entry points are prominent because visitors often arrive knowing a material, yarn type, shade or project need rather than a specific SKU.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Let colour and product context do visual work.

The interface gives products, shade information and catalogue structure enough room to be understood without surrounding them with decorative ecommerce noise.

03 / ENQUIRE

Shorten the path from interest to contact.

Wishlist/enquiry behaviour and WhatsApp sit inside the experience instead of being an afterthought, matching how a wholesale-oriented business actually continues the conversation.

MOBILE / NOT AN AFTERTHOUGHT

THE CATALOGUE
IN ONE HAND.

For this kind of business, mobile cannot be a compressed desktop. The interface keeps search, products and enquiry controls legible and reachable at a narrow viewport, while preserving the same visual identity.

  • Search and navigation remain immediately available
  • Product presentation survives the narrow layout
  • Enquiry actions remain part of the browsing flow
  • Responsive layout is treated as a complete system
WHAT SHIPPED

A REAL SITE.
NOT A
PORTFOLIO PROP.

The final proof is the live website itself: a responsive catalogue, material discovery experience, enquiry list and direct WhatsApp route built around how FakhriMart presents and discusses products with customers.
