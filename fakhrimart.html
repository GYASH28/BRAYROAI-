VERIFIED CLIENT WORK / FAKHRIMART

Catalogue clarity.
Built to enquire.

FakhriMart needed a business website that could make a large visual yarn catalogue feel easier to browse while keeping the path to a real enquiry direct on both desktop and mobile.

ClientFakhriMart
IndustryYarn / textile supply
EngagementBusiness website
StatusLive client project
01 / PROBLEM

Make browsing feel intentional.

The business needed a credible digital presentation for products that are highly visual and varied. A generic corporate layout would have buried the catalogue; a purely decorative site would have made enquiries harder.

The design therefore had to balance identity, catalogue visibility, responsive behavior and a simple route from interest to contact.

02 / BRAYROAI INTERVENTION

Design the journey, not just the page.

Information hierarchyOrganized the experience around what a buyer needs to understand and browse rather than around generic agency-style sections.
Responsive interfaceBuilt desktop and mobile presentations intentionally instead of simply shrinking one layout.
Product presentationUsed real catalogue imagery and visual rhythm to make the inventory feel navigable and premium.
Enquiry pathKept direct contact and product interest close to the browsing experience.
Production deliveryShipped the experience as a real, public website rather than stopping at a design concept.
03 / WHAT IS VERIFIED

Proof without invented metrics.

This page deliberately does not claim revenue, conversion-rate, traffic or ROI improvements because BRAYROAI does not currently have verified source data for those outcomes.

What is verifiable is the live deployment itself: responsive catalogue browsing, desktop/mobile implementation, project-specific visual direction and a direct enquiry-oriented business experience.
