HOW THE ARCHIVE GROWS

Built to outlive the first case study.

01
Verified client work stays separate.

Internal BRAYROAI experiments and concept studies do not get presented as client proof. That keeps the archive useful as more projects are added.

02
Every project carries the same core metadata.

Status, year, sector, location, role, services, case-study route and live URL use one registry structure, so future additions do not need a page redesign.

03
In-progress and upcoming work can exist without pretending it is finished.

The archive supports those states, but a deep case study should only be published once there is enough approved evidence to explain the work properly.

04
No invented outcome metrics.

If a client has not supplied measurable business results, the case study documents the design, system, launch and verified product decisions instead of manufacturing conversion numbers.
