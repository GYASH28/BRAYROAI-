SYSTEMS STARTER KIT / FREE ON-PAGE RESOURCES

Use these before you buy another tool.

The first version of Lab keeps the value directly on the site instead of pretending there is a giant paid academy behind it.

01 / AUDIT

AI Opportunity Audit Checklist

Map repeated work, business value, frequency, data access, human judgment and failure cost before choosing automation.

02 / ACQUISITION

Lead Workflow Map

Trace traffic → capture → qualification → CRM → follow-up → booking → reporting and mark every manual or invisible handoff.

03 / SALES

Micro-Audit Framework

Research one prospect, identify one expensive workflow leak, show the business impact and offer a focused next step instead of a generic cold pitch.

04 / PRICING

Value-Based Scope Checklist

Price around complexity, business value, integration risk, support and ownership — not around how quickly AI helped generate the code.

05 / DELIVERY

Implementation Sequence

Diagnose → map → build → test → launch → measure. Avoid automating ten workflows before one useful system works.

06 / TRUST

Proof Rules

Separate real client work, internal demonstrations and speculative concepts. Never use fake metrics, logos, testimonials or international offices.
