FAQ / REMOVE BUYING RISK

Questions worth answering before the call.

Clear boundaries are part of the service. No guaranteed revenue, fake compliance or mysterious offshore handoff.

Are you an AI agency or a web agency?

BRAYROAI is an implementation studio focused on connected growth and operations systems. Web experience, product design, frontend engineering and AI are capabilities underneath the outcome rather than separate things the client has to coordinate.

Do we have to replace our current website or CRM?

No. The first step is to understand the existing workflow. If the current website, CRM or booking stack can support the desired system, BRAYROAI should integrate with it rather than replace it for novelty.

Can you work with HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Zoho or another CRM?

Integration feasibility depends on the platform, plan, APIs and workflow. BRAYROAI scopes this before promising an implementation and avoids pretending every tool has the same access or capabilities.

How much does an implementation usually cost?

The Growth Engine starts around US$1,500, AI Operations engagements around US$2,500 and more bespoke systems are scoped individually. Final pricing depends on integrations, data, interfaces, automation depth and support.

Can you guarantee more revenue?

No. Revenue depends on demand, offer quality, traffic, sales behavior, pricing and many factors outside a software implementation. BRAYROAI can improve specific system components and measure what changes, not guarantee business outcomes it cannot control.

Are you based in the US or UAE?

No. BRAYROAI is based in Pune, India and is designed for remote international delivery. Region-specific pages adapt the buying and communication journey without implying offices that do not exist.

How do you handle security and privacy?

Access and data requirements are scoped per project. BRAYROAI does not claim certifications such as SOC 2, ISO 27001 or HIPAA compliance unless those standards are genuinely implemented and verified for the specific engagement.

Can we start with a smaller pilot?

Yes. A focused audit or tightly scoped workflow is often a better first engagement than trying to automate an entire company before the highest-value problem is clear.
