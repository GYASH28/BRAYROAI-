## Cross-sample decisions

1. Open with content already readable, then animate its arrangement. The audit repeatedly encountered loaders that concealed the site for more than the short settle window.
2. Build one project-specific peak from real FakhriMart desktop/mobile evidence, rather than mixing unrelated 3D, particles and typographic tricks into every viewport.
3. Use quiet sections after the peak. The strongest examples change pace, surface and scale while keeping type and wayfinding clear.
4. Give the footer a designed role: meaningful next case, contact, route map and resolution. Many awarded projects specifically highlight footer interactions; our version should improve utility as well as visual finish.
5. Treat a feature label, live capture and creator statement as different levels of evidence. The final BRAYROAI site will claim only what its own repository and real case pages prove.
