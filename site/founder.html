BRAYROAI is founder-led by design. Strategy, interface and implementation stay connected from the first conversation to the final browser.

01WHY THE STUDIO EXISTS

Good ideas lose power in the handoff.

When strategy, design and development are separated, the result can become a compromise nobody intended. Yash built BRAYROAI to stay responsible for all three.

The practice is deliberately close and hands-on. Every visual decision has to work as an interaction, at real sizes and speeds, on the device in someone’s hand.
