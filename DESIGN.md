# Material Intelligence design

Gallery objects on deep ink, orange, electric blue and bone grounds. The owner's tile-fracture identity, first-party portraits, actual FakhriMart screens and original arrow give the site a recognisable voice. The signature is an asymmetric luminous particle field that responds to pointer, scroll and a calm-to-orbit range; the emotional peak is the real interface turning from perspective into a front-facing screen.

Each route serves its content: a two-beat particle installation and studio collection; a factual price ledger and scope desk; a client folio; a screen-led case study; a photographic founder feature; an orange workflow map; a blue source-to-answer system; a comfortably indexed agreement. No invented clients, statistics or results.

Typography: self-hosted Space Grotesk display and Manrope text. The approved tile logo keeps its exact geometry at rest. In the footer its tiles respond locally to pointer movement and return to place; keyboard focus moves the ink light. Orange owns impact, blue owns AI/context/primary contact. Contrast and reading order are verified after art direction.

Motion: immediate readable opening with word motion, a brief pinned particle story, a pinned perspective exhibit, lateral capability collection, interactive React practice/plan controls, local parallax, progressive stroked diagrams, ink-arrow feedback, animated menu and native route transitions. No scroll interception or drag-only input. Reduced motion retains stationary full content and all navigation.

Current implementation source: src/site/studio.css and src/site/motion.js. The new owner request supersedes the earlier preserve-the-hero/no-React requirements. Detailed contract: research/material-studio/BRIEF.md.
