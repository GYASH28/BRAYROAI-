# BRAYROAI — design system

## North star

**SIGNAL BECOMING FORM** — scattered digital potential gains hierarchy, discipline, proof, scope, human accountability, and finally identity.

The page should feel like editorial science fiction with human restraint: dark cinematic depth, warm paper, kinetic print composition, exact grids, real work, and motion that explains change. It must not feel like a generic AI agency, crypto launch, gaming site, or card-based template.

## Locked boundary

The loader and `#top` hero are frozen. The remake begins at `.hero-handoff` after the hero closes. See `FROZEN-HERO.md`.

## Palette

- Ink `#08090B` — depth and default dark field
- Bone `#F6F2EA` — readable editorial field and light theme
- Electric blue `#3976FF` — structure, navigation, system logic
- Transformation orange `#FF6428` — decisions, price milestones, action

Blue builds the system. Orange marks a meaningful change.

## Typography

- Space Grotesk — spatial display architecture
- Manrope — practical copy and plan detail
- DM Mono — prices, facts, chapter markers, state labels
- DM Serif Display — rare emotional emphasis

## Motion grammar

- anticipation before a major state change
- action through morph, scale, or typographic interruption
- exact arrival on the editorial grid
- a stable reading hold
- a visual handoff into the next chapter

Native scrolling is the source of progress. One shared runtime updates chapter CSS variables and the persistent WebGL field. Reduced motion resolves every chapter to a stable final frame.

## Anti-template rules

- no generic service or pricing card grids
- no glass panel system, random blobs, fake dashboard, or stock imagery
- no fabricated testimonials, results, clients, awards, or scarcity
- no hover-only information
- no custom cursor dependency or scroll hijacking
- no effect that hides proof, pricing, exclusions, or controls

## Accessibility and performance

- semantic DOM contains every fact and action
- canvas is decorative and `aria-hidden`
- 44px minimum interactive targets where practical
- visible focus, 320px reflow, 200% zoom, and forced-colors support
- WebGL failure and low-power fallbacks
- rendering pauses offscreen, when hidden, when reduced motion is active, or when the visitor pauses ambient motion
