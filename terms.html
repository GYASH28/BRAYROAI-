01 / SERVICES

What BRAYROAI provides.

BRAYROAI provides implementation and creative-technology services that may include the Brayro Growth Engine, AI Operations, workflow audits, conversion websites, CRM and pipeline connections, lead qualification, messaging workflows, booking systems, dashboards, portals, frontend engineering, internal knowledge systems, automation, launch support and related digital work.

Project-specific scope wins.

The exact deliverables are defined in the accepted proposal, quotation, invoice or written scope. If a project-specific agreement conflicts with these general terms on scope, price, timing, ownership or deliverables, the project-specific written agreement takes priority for that engagement.

02 / PRICING & COMMERCIAL SCOPE

Published ranges are starting points, not universal quotes.

BRAYROAI's public website may display indicative international ranges such as Growth Engine implementations from US$1,500, AI Operations engagements from US$2,500 and bespoke systems commonly scoped from US$3,000 upward. These ranges are intended to help buyers qualify fit; they are not binding quotations for every project.

  • Final price depends on workflow complexity, integrations, data access, interface work, required platforms, risk, timeline and support.
  • Regional quotes may be issued in USD, INR, AED or another agreed currency.
  • Ongoing optimization, monitoring or support is separately scoped unless explicitly included in the implementation fee.
  • Third-party subscriptions, messaging usage, model/API usage, hosting, domains and platform fees are separate unless the written proposal says otherwise.

The accepted written scope and quote are the controlling commercial documents for a project.

03 / PAYMENTS

Work starts against an agreed payment schedule.

Payment timing, currency and milestones are stated in the accepted proposal, quotation or invoice. Projects may use an advance, staged payments or a recurring support fee depending on the engagement.

  • Work may pause when an agreed payment becomes overdue.
  • Bank, payment-processor, currency-conversion or international transfer fees may be allocated as stated on the invoice or proposal.
  • Third-party costs are separate unless specifically included.
  • Taxes or statutory charges, where applicable, are additional unless the written quote states otherwise.
04 / SCOPE & REVISIONS

Good systems need a boundary.

The agreed scope defines the workflows, interfaces, integrations, automation, content and support BRAYROAI is responsible for delivering. Reasonable refinements within that scope are part of implementation. Materially new workflows, pages, integrations, datasets, channels, user roles or platform changes are additional scope and may change cost or timing.

Change requests are not a problem.

When a request materially expands the project, BRAYROAI will identify the change before implementing it and can provide a revised price, assumption or timeline.

05 / TIMELINES

Delivery depends on both sides and the systems involved.

Any timeline is based on the agreed scope, timely feedback, access to required accounts/data and the behavior of third-party platforms. Client delays, late approvals, missing credentials, API limitations, third-party outages or major scope changes can move the delivery date. BRAYROAI will communicate material changes to the expected timeline.

For international projects, work may be managed asynchronously with scheduled live overlap for discovery, reviews, decisions and launch. No 24/7 support or foreign office presence is implied unless specifically contracted.

BRAYROAI can configure them where included in scope but cannot guarantee their uptime, pricing, API behavior, policies, model outputs or continued availability.

  • AI-assisted outputs may be probabilistic and should not be treated as infallible human judgment.

  • BRAYROAI does not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA or another certification unless that status is explicitly and verifiably stated for the particular service.
08 / OWNERSHIP & PORTFOLIO

Ownership is made explicit in the project scope.

Unless a project-specific agreement says otherwise, rights to final approved custom deliverables created specifically for the client transfer after all agreed project fees are paid. BRAYROAI retains ownership of pre-existing tools, reusable components, internal methods, templates, general know-how and third-party materials subject to their own licences.

Where a system is built inside a client's own third-party accounts, account ownership remains with the account holder. Where BRAYROAI-hosted or reusable platform components are involved, access and licensing are defined in the project scope.

BRAYROAI may show completed public-facing work in its portfolio or case studies unless confidentiality is agreed in writing. Private data, credentials and confidential workflows should not be published as portfolio proof.

09 / CANCELLATION & REFUNDS

Completed and committed work remains payable.

Either side may end an engagement by written notice, subject to any project-specific commitment. Amounts covering work already completed, committed implementation time, purchased third-party items or a support period that has begun remain payable. Any refund, credit or cancellation arrangement is assessed against the actual work completed and the written project agreement.

10 / WARRANTY & SUPPORT

Launch includes fixing agreed work, not unlimited future changes.

BRAYROAI will correct reproducible defects in delivered work that fall within the agreed scope when reported within the support or warranty period stated in the proposal. New workflows, business-rule changes, new integrations, third-party API changes, model/provider changes, client edits or new functionality are not defects and may require additional work.

To the maximum extent permitted by law, BRAYROAI is not responsible for indirect or consequential losses arising from the project, third-party services, AI/model output, client-provided data/content or events outside reasonable control.
