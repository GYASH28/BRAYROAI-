# BRAYROAI Director's Cut, Focused Edition

**Refined from the owner's direct feedback and full creative authority.**

## Owner correction

- The three smaller offers are monthly care plans after launch, not smaller website-build packages.
- The site had become crowded and the full-screen colour edit between scenes felt ugly.
- Scroll pacing needed to feel smooth and cinematic, with content revealing only after it genuinely enters the viewport.
- Interactions should be numerous enough to feel alive, but optimized and purposeful.
- Only meaningful pages should remain, with a dedicated founder page at the same quality level.

## Direction

- Vibe: bold SaaS film, monochrome fashion portrait, architectural glass, exacting and human.
- World: one dark photographic production space with subtle changes in lighting and depth. No hard orange or cobalt page cuts.
- Journey: hero, connected capabilities, one film peak, real work, clear plan hierarchy, founder, resolve.
- Energy curve: controlled recognition, rising curiosity, one cinematic peak, then trust, clarity, intimacy and resolve.
- Peak: the full-frame BRAYROAI convergence film. It is the only section with a long sticky hold.
- Signature move: the monochrome founder hero reveals its original colour through a pointer-directed aperture and can be locked by the visitor.
- Next action: Start a project.

## Page grammar and score

**Grammar:** restrained cinematic chapters. The earlier rhythmic cutlist was edited down because twelve cuts created continuous intensity and made the site feel crowded.

| Beat | Device | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | pointer aperture + flow | Preserve the liked black-and-white hero and reward entry into the frame |
| System | selector + photographic glass instrument | Combine design, engineering and useful AI without three repetitive service scenes |
| Peak | sticky film + three timed supers | Give the page one memorable cinematic climax |
| Evidence | responsive proof + focus toggle | Replace claims with the real FakhriMart desktop and mobile work |
| Scope | three build surfaces + separate monthly-care rail | Make one-time builds and after-launch care unmistakably different |
| Founder | photographic flow | Restore human scale and link to the dedicated founder story |
| Resolve | focused choice + held CTA | End on one useful project action |

## Feeling curve

1. Recognition: the founder and old hero composition return in monochrome.
2. Coherence: three capabilities behave as one connected instrument.
3. Awe: the convergence film fills the frame and earns the largest scroll span.
4. Trust: real client work settles into view.
5. Clarity: build scope and monthly care are separated by purpose and timing.
6. Intimacy: the founder appears close to the work.
7. Resolve: one project choice changes the actual enquiry.

## Authored restraint

- No full-screen colour flash between scenes.
- No slogan-only interstitials.
- No duplicate service sections.
- Reveals use a 14% intersection threshold and a lower viewport margin, preventing content from appearing while still above the screen.
- The homepage measures about 9.3 viewport-heights on desktop and 9.8 on mobile.

## Meaningful pages

- `/`: focused seven-scene agency experience.
- `/plans`: three one-time website builds, comparison, and clearly separate monthly care.
- `/founder`: founder story, operating principles, method and direct contact.
