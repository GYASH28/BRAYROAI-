# Award-informed design notes

## Study scope

The BRAYROAI research pass dated 30 September 2026 reviewed 50 official Awwwards project records and their public captures: 20 current Sites of the Day, 20 Sites of the Month collection entries and 10 distinct Sites of the Year collection entries. It visually reviewed ten official-poster sheets, ten current live opening/scroll/mobile sheets and seven longer-settled sheets. A scripted audit attempted all 50 current live destinations at desktop and mobile widths; it captured 47 desktop openings, 36 desktop wheel states, 50 mobile openings, 49 mobile wheel states, and delayed revisits of 34 loader-heavy destinations. Read the per-site evidence flags in `live-audit-summary.json` and the interpretations in `interaction-notes.md`; do not claim all 50 complete live interactions were QA-tested.

Official collections: [Day](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/sites_of_the_day/), [Month](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/sites_of_the_month/), [Year](https://www.awwwards.com/websites/sites_of_the_year/). Award category must follow a result collection, not an arbitrary decade/date group of daily winners.

## Transferable findings

- Lead with an image or device the brand actually owns. A worker, athlete, place, product, founder, or real interface is more persuasive than a fashionable background asset.
- Give each motion a job and a rest state. Entry can invite; motion can map, compare, reveal or give scale. Pauses let the visitor read. A perpetual loop has no hierarchy.
- A single, ownable visual relationship is more memorable than a bag of effects. BRAYROAI's arrow can serve as a real control without repeating its previous screen-assembly act.
- Pair experiential movement with a boringly useful route map, strong direct links and a true destination page. Users should be able to refresh, share and reach every study, price or policy independently.
- Show responsive work as responsive work: genuine desktop/mobile captures are evidence. Label the actual viewport and do not imply desktop is an improved version of phone or vice versa.
- Pick color for the depicted subject. The 50 examples range from gray tundra and gallery white to aggressive product red and hand-drawn cartoons; there is no universal award palette.
- Preserve source readability. Experiential headlines can take space, but captions, service facts, legal prose and form controls stay visible, ordinary, high-contrast HTML.
- Premium feel comes from specific art direction, a controlled composition, a material/light story, typography, spatial restraint and precise handling. It does not require blur, frosted cards, moving cursors or WebGL on every route.
- Distinguish browsing evidence from author accounts and from your own live review. Keep Awwwards captures outside production, and use a project's official detail page rather than only an aggregator description.

## Useful primary studies

- [CoMinVi](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/cominvi) is an industrial project. In the live settled/wheel captures, real mining context and dark stone-ground geometry make its orange signal color meaningful. Awwwards highlights additionally label its map, data and 3D moments; those highlight labels are separate evidence.
- [Realevate](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/realevate) ties oversized blue title typography to an actual property frame; live scroll and next-view selection were tested. Transfer the concept of one visual idea making browsing easier, never its look.
- [The Tie-break](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/the-tie-break) uses a sport-specific invitation to introduce a game. The automation remained on its branded loading state; gameplay/shoe moments are official highlight evidence. A game's instruction screen does not justify a long service-site preloader.
- [Anime.js](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/anime-js) puts real animation behaviors into an inspectable tool context, not floating capability cards.
- [Dropbox Brand](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/dropbox-brand) makes a genuine brand language its organizing rule.
- [Igloo Inc.](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/igloo-inc) makes the product world and its medium the actual creative premise. [Awwwards process case study](https://www.awwwards.com/igloo-inc-case-study.html).
- [Don't Board Me](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/dont-board-me) communicates a service with a distinctive single dog image and very direct copy.
- [Opal Tadpole](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/opal-tadpole) uses a hand as real product scale. The physical scale is part of the information.
- [Lusion v3](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/lusion-v3) keeps an experimental visual system coordinated. The creator's [Oryzo AI account on Codrops](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/04/13/lusion-where-digital-craft-meets-ambitious-experimentation/) explains how a single concrete object supports its satellite experiments.
- [OFF+BRAND's creator account](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2026/08/17/creativity-at-enterprise-scale-without-compromise-the-offbrand-story/) describes character-led Lando Norris storytelling and an Aether1 scroll world with an integrated assistant. Their [responsive motion walkthrough](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/12/17/building-responsive-scroll-triggered-curved-path-animations-with-gsap/) recalculates geometry from actual DOM anchors on resize. Borrow the layout-aware principle rather than its subject or library.
- A motion creator's [Codrops interview](https://tympanus.net/codrops/2025/01/10/developer-spotlight-lorenzo-dossi/) describes shaping movement to mood, reducing unnecessary navigation friction and handling the scrollbar layout shift. Treat this as an informed design opinion, not a universal empirical law.

## Resource decisions

- **Anime.js:** official package listed `animejs` v4.5.0, MIT; official docs describe tree-shakeable ESM imports. Use only when its measured timeline, SVG-draw or stagger functionality serves the designed entry or control.
- **Three.js:** official project lists MIT; `npm view` recorded v0.186.1 at the time of research. Confirm version/license again at add time. Keep it local to a single real project display: reduced pixel ratio, visibility and lifecycle guards, on-demand frames, cleanup, still fallback, no fake app screens and no decorative endless loop.
- **Scrollcraft:** the existing project has the Scrollcraft engine, so avoid importing a second scroll controller. Its fingerprint file is a restraint on reusing the same page grammar, route treatment, signature or act sequence.
- Do not append a dependency because it appears in a ranking or a returned library suggestion. Recheck current license and project compatibility before addition; a browser API or small native effect may already fit.
- The project `library-review.md` compares 12 current alternatives, including Motion, OGL, PixiJS, Lenis, Lottie, Barba, Swup, tsParticles, GSAP and Theatre.js. Preserve one owner for narrative scroll state and one normalized input value for the actual proof transformation.
