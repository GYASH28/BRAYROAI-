01 / WHY THE STUDIO EXISTS

Good ideas lose power in the handoff.

When strategy, design and development are separated, the result can become a compromise nobody intended. BRAYROAI is built to keep the thinking and the making in the same room.

The practice is deliberately close and hands-on. Every visual decision has to work as an interaction at real sizes, speeds and input methods.

YASH GANESH · PUNE, INDIA

02 / THE OPERATING PRINCIPLES

What the work
has to earn.

01

Clarity before style.

Know the decision the visitor needs to make before deciding how the page looks.

02

Craft through code.

Art direction has to survive its way into a working, responsive browser.

03

Technology with a use.

Motion and AI earn their place when they help someone understand, choose or act.

03 / THE METHOD

Direction and delivery in one loop.

  1. 01
    Find the real problem.

    Start with the business, the audience and the decision the site needs to make easier.

  2. 02
    Build the point of view.

    Shape hierarchy, language, image, motion and interaction as one system.

  3. 03
    Prove it in the browser.

    Implement, test and refine until the idea works at real sizes and speeds.
