01 / THE BUSINESS CONTEXT

A changing catalogue needs an honest interface.

FakhriMart serves customers looking for yarns, embroidery materials, macrame cord, beads, bases, purse materials and other craft supplies. Some buyers need a single item; others buy for resale or in bulk.

Current shades, packs, stock and quantity prices can change. A fixed-price checkout would have implied certainty the business still needs to confirm personally.

02 / THE DESIGN CHALLENGE

Help people arrive at a better question.

The site needed to organise a large range, give buyers enough context to choose a material, and carry useful details into a WhatsApp enquiry.

01

Browse by material.

Clear product families provide a useful first step for visitors who know the craft but not the exact product name.

02

Show verified context.

Product and use information helps buyers narrow options without promising live stock or exact shades the site cannot verify.

03

Make enquiry intentional.

The interface gathers product context before the conversation, so the store can confirm the variable details directly.

03 / DELIVERED

A useful digital front door.

BRAYRO AI shaped the information architecture, responsive interface, catalogue discovery and enquiry flow, then built and launched the website.

The live result is observable. This case study does not claim measured conversion or revenue gains.
