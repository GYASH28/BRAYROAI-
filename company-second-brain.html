FAQ

Before we connect
anything.

The useful questions are about scope, ownership and how the system behaves when it does not know.

01Do you copy our entire Drive into the system?

No. The starting point is an approved content set. A live Drive integration is expanded scope and should be limited to the folders and access model agreed for the use case.

02Can different departments see different information?

Yes, but role-based or department-specific permissions add architecture and testing complexity, so they are scoped separately from the simplest base implementation.

03Can it connect to WhatsApp or our CRM?

Potentially. Drive, CRM, WhatsApp and API connections are explicitly listed as expanded scope because feasibility, permissions, provider capabilities and workflow requirements differ by client.

04Will the assistant always be correct?

No AI system can be honestly promised as always correct. The design goal is to ground answers in approved information, test representative questions, surface source context where supported, and make uncertainty or missing knowledge visible.

05What happens after the 14-day support period?

You can continue with the delivered system under the agreed setup, request separately scoped changes, or use Knowledge Care from ₹2,999/mo for agreed updates, review and maintenance.

06Do we need the AI Workflow Audit first?

Not always, but if the use case is unclear, the ₹9,999 Workflow Audit is the cleaner first step. If that audit leads to a BRAYROAI implementation, its fee is credited toward the build.
