# Creative direction — Signal Becoming Form

## Thesis and differentiation

BRAYROAI is not presented as a menu of agency services. One persistent signal changes state as the visitor gains confidence: residual lattice → designed page → attention planes → four connected disciplines → proof frame → scope thresholds → founder halo → BRAYROAI identity.

Removing the logo still leaves a recognizable system: oversized cropped typography, thin editorial construction lines, blue as structure, orange as decision, asymmetric 12-column compositions, and one material that transforms rather than resetting between sections.

## Design freedom score

DFII: **13/15**

- typography: 3/3
- composition: 3/3
- motion continuity: 3/3
- interaction: 2/3
- material/technical expression: 2/3

The missing two points are deliberate restraint: real business proof and price comprehension must remain more important than experimentation.

## Particle direction

The signal is a cinematic material, not background confetti. It continuously interpolates between chapter centres and carries the page's meaning: a dimensional possibility grid, the word `SEEN`, four connected discipline nodes, browser and phone proof frames, three scope gates, a human contour and halo, and the resolved BRAYROAI wordmark. A two-pass WebGL treatment separates soft atmospheric glow from crisp signal cores. Pointer pressure, press ripples, service choices, proof-view choices, and plan focus all affect the same field without taking control away from native scrolling.

## Belief map

| Chapter | Visitor belief change | Handoff |
|---|---|---|
| Potential | Good presentation can start affordably | page rails become type planes |
| Attention | Hierarchy determines what people notice | focal points become clusters |
| Disciplines | Direction can survive implementation | one stream becomes a browser edge |
| Proof | The studio has real work to inspect | proof rails become scope thresholds |
| Scope | Price, boundary, and next step are understandable | orange marker becomes portrait rule |
| Studio | A real person stays close to the work | halo becomes letter field |
| Action | The next step is direct and low-friction | particles resolve into identity |

## Responsive cut

Desktop uses deep spatial scale and bounded sticky reading moments. Tablet collapses depth planes and keeps the proof nearly frontal. Mobile removes pinning, stacks every semantic control, reduces particle count, and presents the same narrative in ordinary document flow. Reduced motion renders the resolved art direction with the WebGL canvas disabled.
