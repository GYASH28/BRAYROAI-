This page explains what BRAYRO AI uses when you browse the site, choose a market, contact the studio or talk to Rae. The goal is to collect as little as practical for the job.

Analytics and performance

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to understand page traffic and real-world performance such as Core Web Vitals. These are first-party measurements served through this site. BRAYRO AI does not use advertising trackers or sell visitor data.

Conversion events may record actions such as opening a plan, changing market, visiting a case study or starting an enquiry. The event describes the action, not the text you type into your project brief.

Market preferences

If you manually choose India, the UAE or Australia, the site stores that preference in local storage and a same-site cookie so the correct price book can be shown on later visits. Automatic market detection is used only as a starting suggestion and does not request precise device location.

Project enquiries

The rough project brief stays in your browser session while you edit it. When you choose WhatsApp or email, your browser opens that service with the message you chose to send. BRAYRO AI receives the enquiry through the service you selected.

Please do not include passwords, API keys, financial credentials or other secrets in a first enquiry.

Rae, the site assistant

When you open Rae and send a message, the text and limited conversation context needed to answer are sent to BRAYRO AI's configured AI service. Rae is intended for questions about the studio, services and project fit; do not use it for confidential information.

Third-party services

The site may link to GitHub, Vercel-hosted projects, WhatsApp and email. Those services have their own privacy practices. Studio-system case studies can load public screenshots directly from GitHub's raw-content service.

Your control

You can clear browser storage at any time to remove saved market and draft preferences. You can also disable Vercel Web Analytics for this browser by using the control below.
