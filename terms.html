Plain-English operating terms.

By approving a BRAYROAI proposal, paying an invoice, starting a monthly plan or otherwise instructing BRAYROAI to begin work, the client agrees to these terms together with any project-specific written scope.

01 / SERVICES

What BRAYROAI provides.

BRAYROAI provides creative technology services that may include website strategy, design, frontend development, content structure, motion, useful AI workflows, implementation, launch support and related digital work. The exact deliverables are defined in the accepted proposal, plan description, written scope or invoice.

Project-specific scope wins.

If a signed proposal or written quotation conflicts with these general terms on scope, price, timing or deliverables, the project-specific written agreement takes priority for that engagement.

02 / PRICING & PLANS

Monthly and one-time work are different.

BRAYROAI offers both ongoing monthly website partnerships and one-time website builds. Monthly plans cover recurring work within an agreed monthly scope. One-time builds cover a defined project from agreed start through delivery.

  • Monthly plans currently start at ₹2,599/month, ₹3,999/month and ₹5,999+/month depending on ongoing scope.
  • One-time website builds currently start at ₹9,999, ₹17,999 and approximately ₹25,000–₹35,000+ for premium/custom work.
  • Custom integrations, ecommerce, large catalogues, advanced AI, paid third-party services and unusually large content work may require separate pricing.

Displayed website prices are starting points. Final scope and price may be confirmed in writing before work begins.

03 / PAYMENTS

Work starts against an agreed payment schedule.

Payment timing is stated in the accepted proposal, quotation or invoice. Monthly plans are ordinarily billed for the upcoming service period. One-time projects may use staged payments or an advance before work begins.

  • Work may pause when an agreed payment becomes overdue.
  • Third-party costs such as domains, hosting, paid plugins, stock assets or platform fees are separate unless specifically included.
  • Taxes or statutory charges, where applicable, are additional unless the written quote states otherwise.
04 / SCOPE & REVISIONS

Good projects need a boundary.

The agreed scope defines what BRAYROAI is responsible for delivering. Reasonable refinements within that scope are part of the design process. Materially new pages, features, integrations, design directions, content volumes or workflows are treated as additional scope and may change cost or timing.

Change requests are not a problem.

When a request materially expands the project, BRAYROAI will identify the change before implementing it and can provide a revised price or timeline.

05 / TIMELINES

Delivery depends on both sides.

Any timeline is based on the scope, timely feedback and availability of required content or access. Client delays, late approvals, missing assets, third-party outages or major scope changes can move the delivery date. BRAYROAI will communicate material changes to the expected timeline.

The client supplies what only the client can supply.

The client is responsible for providing accurate business information, lawful content, brand assets, product data, approvals, account access and feedback needed to complete the work. The client confirms that material supplied to BRAYROAI can legally be used in the project.

07 / THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

External platforms have their own rules.

Websites may rely on hosting providers, domains, analytics, email services, APIs, payment providers, fonts, plugins or other third-party systems. BRAYROAI can configure these where included in scope, but cannot guarantee uninterrupted operation, pricing or policies of services controlled by third parties.

08 / OWNERSHIP & PORTFOLIO

Approved work transfers after payment.

Unless a project-specific agreement says otherwise, the client receives rights to the final approved custom website deliverables created specifically for the client after all agreed project fees are paid. BRAYROAI retains ownership of pre-existing tools, reusable components, internal methods, templates, know-how and third-party materials subject to their own licences.

BRAYROAI may show completed public-facing work in its portfolio, case studies or promotional materials unless confidentiality is agreed in writing before publication.

09 / CANCELLATION & REFUNDS

Completed work remains payable.

Either side may end an engagement by written notice, subject to any project-specific commitment. Amounts covering work already completed, committed time, purchased third-party items or a service period that has begun remain payable. Any refund, credit or cancellation arrangement is assessed against the actual work completed and the written project agreement.

Unless a proposal says otherwise, monthly work can be stopped before the next billing period rather than renewing into another month.

10 / WARRANTY & SUPPORT

Launch includes fixing agreed work, not unlimited future changes.

BRAYROAI will correct reproducible defects in the delivered work that fall within the agreed scope when reported within a reasonable period after delivery. New content, browser/platform changes, third-party service changes, client edits or new functionality are not defects and may require maintenance or additional work.
