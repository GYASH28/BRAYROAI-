BUYER QUESTIONS

What a skeptical US buyer should know.

Clarity is part of the pitch.

Are you a US company?

No. BRAYROAI is based in Pune, India and works remotely. The page is localized for US buyers without pretending there is a US office or legal presence.

Who owns the code and workflows?

Ownership and account access should be written into the project scope. BRAYROAI's default commercial direction is to avoid unnecessary lock-in and make the implemented system understandable to the client.

Can you work with our existing CRM?

Often, yes, but feasibility depends on the platform, subscription tier, APIs and current configuration. Integration is verified during scoping before being promised.

Do you guarantee more sales?

No. BRAYROAI can improve parts of the acquisition and operations system, but cannot guarantee revenue because demand, offer, traffic and sales execution sit outside a software build.
