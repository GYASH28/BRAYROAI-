# BRAYRO AI design direction

The studio's current design system is an editorial sketchbook: warm paper, deep ink, an orange signal, strong typography, purposeful whitespace and authored visual diagrams. The original dark, portrait-led hero stays as the opening identity moment by explicit user request. From the work scene onward, each section should help a visitor understand evidence, process, the founder, scope or the next step.

## Rules

- Keep the hero headline and Yash portrait recognisable. The colour toggle must remain functional.
- Show actual client work early and label the claims that can be verified. Do not invent results, testimonials or project outcomes.
- Use motion to explain the clarify → create → connect sequence. All content remains visible when scripts or motion enhancements are unavailable.
- Mobile is composed for its own width. Maintain readable text and controls at 360 and 390 px.
- Rae is a small opt-in companion with her original ivory shell, dark visor, orange accents and glowing eyes. The character should not obscure the work.
- Prices are independent, market-specific books in English. Scope and exclusions remain near the price.
- The terms page belongs to the same visual system but prioritizes reading comfort.

The current source of truth for selectors and tokens is `src/site/site.css`. Implementation context and verification limits are in `docs/REBUILD_NOTES.md`.
